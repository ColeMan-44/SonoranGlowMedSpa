01 / INJECTABLES

A little refresh.
Still entirely you.

Precision meets possibility. Explore thoughtful ways to soften lines, restore volume, and enhance your natural features.

Botox & Dysport

Neuromodulators relax targeted muscles to soften the appearance of expression lines. Kym personalizes treatment to your facial anatomy and goals, with an emphasis on subtle, natural-looking results.

Common areas include vertical frown lines, horizontal forehead lines, crow’s feet, and bunny lines around the nose. A consultation can also explore a lip flip, a downturned smile, platysmal neck bands, or excessive underarm sweating.

Juvéderm & Restylane dermal fillers

Hyaluronic acid-based fillers add volume and contour while supporting a hydrated appearance. A personalized plan can help restore facial balance and enhance your existing features.

Treatment areas may include the cheeks, chin, lips, vertical lip lines, jawline, marionette lines, and nasolabial folds.

PDO thread lifting

PDO (polydioxanone) threads are absorbable sutures used to lift and support sagging tissue while encouraging collagen production.

Changes may be visible initially and continue to develop over time. This nonsurgical option generally involves less downtime than surgical lifting; suitability and recovery are discussed during your consultation.

02 / WELLNESS

A moment to restore.
A little care within.

Thoughtfully selected nutrients. Individual attention. Explore wellness options built around your needs and your story.

Nutritional IV therapy

Nutritional IV therapy delivers fluids, vitamins, and minerals directly into the bloodstream. Kym can discuss available formulations and whether an infusion is appropriate for your wellness goals.

Whether your interest is hydration, workout recovery, or general wellness, start with a conversation about your health history and individual needs.

Vitamin B12 injections

B12 supports normal nerve function and red blood cell production. Injections may be considered when supplementation is appropriate, including for people with a B12 deficiency.

If fatigue, brain fog, or low energy is a concern, discuss it with Kym so your care reflects your health needs rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.

Lipotropic injections

Sometimes called “The Skinny Shot,” lipotropic injections contain a blend of methionine, inositol, choline, and B12.

Learn how these ingredients fit into a personalized wellness plan. Your consultation is a chance to discuss goals, expected benefits, and suitability; weight-loss outcomes are not guaranteed.

03 / REJUVENATION

Fresh skin.
Fresh possibilities.

Stay in the glow with skin-focused treatments and thoughtful home care. A little science, a little self-care, and a plan made for you.

Microneedling with AnteAGE or PRF

Microneedling creates controlled microchannels in the skin to support its natural renewal process and improve the appearance of texture and fine lines.

Kym can discuss options incorporating AnteAGE products or your own platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) as part of your treatment plan.

Chemical peel facials

A personalized peel helps exfoliate the skin and refresh the appearance of dullness, uneven texture, and visible signs of aging.

The type of peel and aftercare are selected with your skin, concerns, and goals in mind.

LED light therapy & dermaplaning

Explore LED light therapy as part of your skin care plan, or dermaplaning to gently remove surface buildup and fine facial hair.

Kym can help you choose an individual treatment or a complementary approach tailored to your skin.

PRX-T33 • the “unicorn peel”

This professional bio-revitalization treatment combines trichloroacetic acid (TCA), hydrogen peroxide, and kojic acid. It is designed to refresh the appearance of the skin and support a brighter, firmer-looking complexion.

Sessions typically take around 30 minutes and are designed to minimize visible peeling. Concerns to discuss include dullness, uneven pigmentation such as melasma, acne scarring, enlarged pores, fine lines, and changes in firmness. Kym will assess your skin type, treatment areas, and suitability.

Alastin Skincare

Continue your care beyond the treatment room with Alastin Skincare products, available at Sonoran Glow Med Spa.

Discover science-led skin care and a routine selected to support your skin goals and complement your professional treatments.

04 / REGENERATIVE

Your own resources.
A renewed glow.

Explore PRF and EZ GEL: personalized treatments that use components of your own blood to support skin renewal.

PRF & EZ GEL facial rejuvenation

Platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) and EZ GEL are prepared using components of your own blood. These treatments are used to support collagen production and tissue renewal for a refreshed appearance.

A consultation can explore concerns such as skin texture, fine lines, the tear trough area beneath the eyes, and lines around the mouth. Your plan prioritizes individual needs, comfort, and appropriate care.

PRF with microneedling

Pair microneedling with PRF as part of a skin renewal plan focused on texture and overall complexion.

Kym will explain the treatment process, expected recovery, and how this option fits your skin care goals.

Hair restoration

PRF can also be considered as part of a personalized approach to hair restoration.

Discuss your concerns and health history with Kym to understand whether regenerative treatment may be appropriate and what outcomes you can reasonably expect.
