A LITTLE CLARITY, BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Good questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Curious about your first visit? Start here, then bring your questions to Kym.

Ask us a question ↗
What happens at my first consultation?

Your complimentary consultation is a chance to share your goals and concerns with Kym, review your health history, and explore suitable treatment options. Bring your questions — you don’t need to know which treatment you want before we talk.

Do I need to decide on a treatment right away?

Your consultation starts with information and a personalized conversation. You can ask about options, expected outcomes, and next steps before deciding what feels right for you.

How should I prepare for my appointment?

Preparation depends on the treatment. Ask Kym for instructions specific to your visit and share any medications, supplements, allergies, and recent procedures. Do not stop prescribed medication without guidance from your prescribing clinician.

Will I need downtime?

Recovery varies by treatment and person. Some treatments may involve temporary redness, swelling, or bruising. Kym can explain expected downtime and help you plan around work, activities, or upcoming events.

What about aftercare?

Aftercare is tailored to your treatment and skin. Ask for your specific instructions, including skin care, sun protection, activity restrictions, and how to follow up with questions.

Can I book or send a message on this demo site?

You can try the Name, Email, and Message form, but demo submissions are not delivered or saved. The phone number, address, hours, and social links are placeholders. Live booking and real contact details will be added before launch.

How does the first-time client offer work?

The 15% off banner is a demo offer for now. Offer eligibility, exclusions, and final terms will be confirmed before live booking opens.
